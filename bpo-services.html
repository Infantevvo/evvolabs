Business Process Outsourcing

What We Do in

BPO


We provide comprehensive, integrated business process outsourcing services so that our clients can save on upfront costs and focus solely on their core competencies and leave both back office and front office work in our care.

This way we assist our client to have time flexibility and direct quality control as we provide consistent quality, fast turnaround time, and top-notch resources without the need to invest in any assets thereby converting fixed costs to variable costs.

Flexible and Knowledgable Workers

Skilled resources with domain knowledge, flexible project based on demand and specific services support with qualified project team.

Unique Value Proposition

We help our clients to optimize their costs especially in technical resources, expertise evaluation and third-party threat assessment.

Staffing Expertise

We have a pool of full-fledged and variance team consisting of QAs, data engineers, cloud architects, technical project managers as well as DevOps experts on board.

BPO Consulting Expertise

We are always ready to provide free consultation and advice on functions you find limiting and hindrance to your operational and execution growth. Over the years, we have mobilized warm bodies and outsourced team members either remotely and even to clients’ offices to sort out resource issue albeit temporary or long term. Our foremost interest lie in solving your human capital needs so that you can continue with your core business.

Our Approach

Our streamlined process for any type of offshore collaborations will ensure you get exactly what you need.

Engagement &
Delivery Models

Our self-managed teams work during your business calendar and hours, receive your priorities, execute and adhere to strict SLAs and ensure your business goals are supported the way you want them to be.





Fixed Cost Model


We begin a new project with an assurance of completing the project within the set timeline and at the budget given. Outsourcing your business process to our dedicated resource at a fixed monthly rate assure you savings on your overall overhead costs almost immediately.


Hybrid


This is a combination of fixed price and time and materials model. A very popular model as it balances both static and dynamic parts of the project which most clients prefer.


Build Operate & Transfer


We build offshore facility for client and operate and transfer complete ownership upon stability of operations and maturity of contract.


Time & Material


This is a simple model where we charge by per hour billing and the fixed hourly rate can be locked at certain rate for bulk contract.
