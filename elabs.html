Our
success story


Since 2008, EVVO has become successful in information technology. Our enthusiasm and deep passion for anything from digital transformation to data assets and the quest for learning, developing and deploying innovative solutions have earned us the trust from government agencies to SMEs alike.



2008

1

Founded EVVO Media

EVVO Media incorporated as a video streaming and hosting company. It gradually evolved as a technology company to provide video solutions for early believers and adopters of video power.

2009

2

Launched Proprietary EVVO Products

EVVO built its own proprietary EVVO streaming platform, EVVO Channel & EVVO Live.

2010

3

First Government Tender Win For Digital Media

EVVO was awarded winner of IDA (T) 828 EnVision 1st Video Hosting and Streaming Bulk Tender. This award enabled us to supply to all government agencies for services provisioned under the tender.

2011

4

Established EVVO Labs

Due to growing demands for new technology requests from government agencies and clients, Labs was incorporated to do more R&D and implementation of convergent technology for the next generation of businesses.

2014

5

Consecutive Tender Wins For Digital Media

Awarded consecutive 2nd tender win of IDA (T) 1152 EnVision Video Hosting and Streaming Bulk Tender from 2014 to 2018.

2014

6

New Government Tender Win For CyberSecurity

In a remarkable year, we were awarded the 1st CyberSecurity Bulk Tender IDA (T) 1269 for DDOS Mitigation Services from 2014 to 2017.

2015

7

Attained EPPU 9 Status

As an emerging force in IT SME sector, we achieved Government EPPU S9 status which qualify us to bid for any Gebiz project in Singapore to the tune of up to $30 million.

2016

8

Video Streaming Tender Award

Awarded 3rd consecutive Video Hosting and Streaming Bulk Tender Win Under IDA (T) 1378 EnVision.

2016

9

Achieved MTCS Certification

EVVO achieved MTCS certification.

2016

10

Attained ISO 27001

Attained ISO 27001 signifying our commitment to excellence.

2017

11

CyberSecurity Tender Award

Awarded 2nd consecutive CyberSecurity Bulk Tender Win Under GovTech (T) 16105 DDOS Mitigation Services.

2018

12

IOT Regional Logistics Win

EVVO awarded auto sortation project for WMS implementation on a regional basis with each sortation plant managing over 100k parcels per day.

2019

13

Established India Office

EVVO started an office in Bangalore to provide Business Processing Outsourcing work to ever increasing demand from clients all over the region.

2020

14

BizSafe Certification Environment

2020

15

Going DPTM Way

EVVO embarking on the new journey of Data Protection Trust Mark. This is our journey toward becoming a data centric company that our clients can trust.
