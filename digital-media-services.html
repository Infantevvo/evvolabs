Digital Media Consulting


Being captain of the industry since the day we first won the IDA Digital Media Bulk Tender award more than a decade ago, EVVO has been proactive in building its own proprietary products that have been used by most government agencies and education institutes in Singapore.

We are very good in what we do here, be it streaming your content to multiple locations, providing classroom or training platform to conduct classes or courses and even broadcasting national level events to any IP devices.

Till this day we remain faithful to our vision and belief in the power of digital media marketing and with the continuous increase in internet penetration; we can help you to manage and deliver your content to any part of the world whilst making them accessible to audience who otherwise would not have been able to reach without our solutions.
