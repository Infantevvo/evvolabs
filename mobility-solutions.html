Mobility Solutions

Reach your users,anytime,anywhere, on any device


Mobility obstacles that organizations face are lack of expertise, data security concerns, support for multi-platform Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) initiatives. This is where EVVO mobility products come into play. We help bring you more workplace productivity, greater customer engagement, and deeper partner alliances through our mobility solutions.

EVVO’s mobility services integrate with business processes to deliver engaging, easy-to-use mobile solutions. Our designs are device and technology independent and will make your users stay connected on any platform securely.

Mobility Consulting Services

Our mobility consulting framework helps you transform your business processes and become a future-ready mobile venture. We develop mobility strategies, mobility channel design, application blueprints and device planning for companies who are not mobile friendly and make your business not only more competitive but also more successful in a vast mobile environment.

Mobile App Development

We turn your ideas into fruition once we start our research and wireframing the future functionalities with high emphasis on user experience. Along with the technical feasibility assessment, we build a prototype and test out the design whilst our UX architect the interaction between design elements and the user interface (UI) designer builds the look and feel of your app. This is a multistep process with its many review stages and as development stage progresses, we continue to test to satisfaction before UAT and deployment of application.

Enterprise Mobility

Our Enterprise Mobility offer remote working options and allow the use of personal laptops and mobile devices for business purposes and make use of cloud technology for data access. EVVO recognizes the need for greater agility brought on by a shift from the traditional central office to remote model. This shift includes the security of remote and mobile devices, as well as providing employees the devices and support needed to enable mobility. Along with managing device-level access, we also help IT admins control access by application, file, employee role, location amongst other factors. Enterprises can increase access to digital assets while still maintaining proprietary control amidst high standards of security and governance.

Mobility Infrastructure

We plan new mobility infrastructure on top of existing legacy systems to enable flexibility and scalability option as more and more large enterprises are embracing BYOD model with increasing attention to MDM/MAM mobility backend as a service (MBAS) to reduce capex.

Our Approach

To meet our customers’ ever-changing goals and objectives, we develop an agile and robust mobility platform that is suitable for any environment.
