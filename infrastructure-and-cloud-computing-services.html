Infrastructure and cloud computing

What We Do

We managed our very own MTCS certified cloud environment along with our infra partner as well as offering a fully managed suite of the world’s best cloud services and solutions from companies such as Microsoft, VMWare, OpenStack, AliCloud, and HP to name a few.

Our scalable solutions allow users to access data, applications and services over both intranet and internet as well as on the go thereby eliminating the need for expensive hardware and servers thereby reducing capex costs.

We make it easy for your organization to deploy and modernize your infrastructure at the most competitive pricing and provide you with the tools to enable your team to work anywhere anytime without worries knowing that we have your back covered with our 24/7 managed support.

Infrastructure As A Service

We have virtual servers for Windows and Linux platforms that come in high speed private connectivity and high service availability of up to 99.99%. We also offer scalability on demand.

BackUp As A Service

We provide scheduled backups to EVVO private cloud including incremental backup and secured connectivity via SSL VPN. We have the most reliable backup solution to protect your data from leakages.

Diaster Recovery As a Service & Work Area Recovery

We offer flexible recovery options for both bare metal and fully loaded server and rapid boot up recovery image under our VM. Our customized recovery procedures with hosting redundancy keep your data safe under our end to end business continuity plan.

Managed Services

We provide entire gamut of managed services for internet connectivity with wireless LAN, SD WAN and link balancing and back up from end point to cloud PC.
