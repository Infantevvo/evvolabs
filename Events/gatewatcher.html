Evvo Labs and Gatewatcher announce Strategic Partnership

Singapore, 6 December 2023 - Evvo Labs, a leader in Managed Security Services, and Gatewatcher, leader in network-based cyber threat detection, today announced a strategic partnership to enhance cybersecurity measures across Singapore's various industries. This collaboration aims to combat the evolving threat landscape, including zero-day exploits, by integrating Evvo Labs' expertise in managed security with Gatewatcher's advanced NDR solution.

The partnership between Evvo Labs and Gatewatcher unites the unique strengths of both companies: Gatewatcher's advanced Network Detection and Response (NDR) solution merge seamlessly with Evvo Labs' expertise in managed security services. This strategic integration aims to establish a comprehensive cybersecurity framework, aligning perfectly with Gatewatcher's international expansion strategy.

This collaboration is a significant milestone in addressing the cybersecurity needs of Singapore's government and private sectors. By reducing complexity in managing cybersecurity systems, this partnership enables businesses to concentrate on their growth and innovation with robust security measures.

In today's digital era, where an online presence and data security are crucial, this partnership has added significance. By pooling their resources and expertise, Evvo Labs and Gatewatcher are delivering holistic cybersecurity solutions, adept at navigating the increasingly complex landscape of cyber threats.

Speaking about the partnership, Cristofer Quek, Regional Sales, APAC, Gatewatcher, said, "Government and private sector enterprises are confronted with the rise of advanced persistent threats, requiring a more circumspect, probing attitude that looks for the early signs of larger, longer-term attacks. Our partnership with Evvo Labs is a strategic move to bolster defenses of companies in the APAC region. By helping them integrate and define our NDR solution as a pillar of their cyber strategy, we aim to provide enhanced visibility and a proactive defense approach, crucial for mitigating the impact of cyber-attacks.

Echoing this sentiment, Ken-Jin, CTO of Evvo Labs, reflected on the significance of this alliance, saying, 'In today's fast-paced digital landscape, robust cybersecurity is not a luxury but a necessity for businesses of all sizes. Our partnership with Gatewatcher marks a significant stride towards democratizing advanced cybersecurity solutions. We are committed to equipping businesses, especially those facing budget constraints, with powerful yet affordable defenses against the increasingly sophisticated cyber threats.

For more information on this partnership and the services offered by Evvo Labs and Gatewatcher, please contact info@evvolabs.com
